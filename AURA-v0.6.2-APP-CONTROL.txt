AURA v0.6.2 App Control

This update fixes commands such as:
- Open YouTube
- Please open Chrome
- Could you launch Gmail please
- Open WhatsApp
- Open Google Maps
- Open Spotify
- Open any other installed launcher app by its visible label

What changed:
- Explicit low-risk app-opening commands execute immediately.
- Background wake commands no longer create approval cards for opening apps.
- Sensitive actions such as messages, email, calls, reminders, calendar changes,
  and sharing still require approval.
- App names are normalized, including "You Tube", "YT", polite command forms,
  and common aliases.
- Known Android package names and generic launcher-label discovery are both used.
- YouTube and supported web services fall back to their secure website when the
  native app is not installed.
- App-control execution is recorded in AURA's private audit log.
- Logcat tags: AuraActionExecutor and AuraBackgroundAction.

No Gemini or Groq key is changed by this update.
