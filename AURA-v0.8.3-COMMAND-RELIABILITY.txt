AURA v0.8.3 Command Reliability

Fixes:
- Stops Whisper from transcribing silence as Korean, Tamil or other unsupported scripts.
- Rejects unsupported-script hallucinations in Auto speech mode.
- Preserves English, Bengali and Hindi transcripts.
- Collapses duplicated voice transcripts such as "my battery level is my battery level".
- Understands "open YouTube and play <title>" and similar compound commands.
- Corrects "your tube", "you tube", "u tube" and "you toob" to YouTube.
- Prevents the generic app opener from treating a full compound command as an app name.
- Recognizes battery-level and battery-percentage phrases locally.
- Removes literal Markdown bold/heading markers from optional AI replies.
- Adds redacted YouTube resolver diagnostics without logging API keys.
