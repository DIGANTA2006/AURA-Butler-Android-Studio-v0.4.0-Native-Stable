AURA v0.8.2 — Free News and 10-Day Weather
================================================

Version name: 0.8.2-free-news-weather
Version code: 12

NEWS COMMANDS
-------------
Tell me the news
- Reads the latest numbered headlines only.

Briefly tell me the news
Brief technology news
- Reads each headline with one short factual description.

Tell me detailed news
Tell me full news about West Bengal
- Uses Gemini grounded research when enabled.
- Without Gemini, shows the best description supplied by the news feed and source links.

Explain headline one
Briefly explain the second headline
Tell me the third news in detail
- Expands a story from AURA's most recently saved encrypted headline list.

FREE NEWS SOURCES
-----------------
1. Optional GNews free API
   - Add a GNews API key in Settings > Live news and weather.
   - The key is stored in encrypted preferences.
   - Free-tier delivery and usage limits are controlled by GNews.

2. Keyless fallback
   - Google News RSS for India is used automatically when no GNews key is set,
     when the free quota is exhausted, or when the API call fails.

AURA does not copy or scrape full publisher articles. It provides a summary,
source, publication time, and link. Gemini grounded research can create a
verified multi-source explanation when optional AI and research are enabled.

WEATHER COMMANDS
----------------
What is the weather?
What is the weather in Kolkata?
Tell me the 10-day weather forecast
Give me a ten-day forecast for Kolkata
Weather for next week
Show a 14-day forecast

Open-Meteo requires no API key for this private build. Forecasts support 3 to
16 days and include:
- condition
- minimum and maximum temperature
- apparent temperature range
- rain probability and expected precipitation
- maximum wind speed

COMMAND CORRECTION
------------------
The local normalizer now repairs common forms including:
bews/newz -> news
headine -> headline
weathre/wether -> weather
forcast/forecat -> forecast
befley/brifly -> briefly

PRIVACY
-------
- GNews key is encrypted on the phone.
- Recent headline metadata is stored encrypted for follow-up commands.
- Article pages are not silently downloaded or copied.
- Weather calls send only the requested coordinates or place query.
