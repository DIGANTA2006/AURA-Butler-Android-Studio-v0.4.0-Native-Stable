AURA v0.9.0 Production Intelligence Foundation

This release does not claim to train a Siri/Google-scale foundation model. It upgrades AURA into a safer hybrid personal-assistant architecture:
- deterministic on-device actions and approval policy
- encrypted memory
- optional private OpenAI-compatible local brain
- Gemini and Groq fallback
- private-context sharing disabled by default
- prompt-injection boundaries for analyzed data
- bounded HTTP responses and endpoint validation
- centralized professional butler behavior
- response repetition/format quality gate
- local-only crash journal
- localhost-only cleartext exception for ADB reverse

Recommended private PC model: qwen3:4b.
