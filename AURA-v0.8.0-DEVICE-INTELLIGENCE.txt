AURA v0.8.0 — Device Intelligence and Smart Butler

Version
- versionCode: 10
- versionName: 0.8.0-device-intelligence

Included capabilities
1. Device audio control
   - Media, ringtone, alarm, notification, call and system streams.
   - Raise, lower, mute, unmute, minimum, maximum and percentage commands.

2. One-shot screenshots
   - Commands such as “take a screenshot” and “save this screen”.
   - Android asks for screen-sharing consent for each capture session.
   - Saves the approved frame in Pictures/AURA on Android 10 and later.

3. Screen and document understanding
   - Read, transcribe, summarize, translate, explain and analyze visible text.
   - Corrects only obvious OCR/spelling mistakes and marks unclear text instead of inventing it.
   - Preserves names, numbers, dates, URLs, codes and technical terms.

4. Thermal status and Cooling Assist
   - Reports battery percentage, battery-temperature sensor, charging state,
     Android thermal status and Battery Saver state when available.
   - Cooling Assist can reduce display brightness when system-settings permission
     is granted and opens Battery Saver controls.
   - It does not claim to control sealed hardware, fans or the phone's firmware.

5. Safe cache controls
   - Cleans AURA's temporary cache without deleting settings, private memory,
     notification history or Gemini/Groq keys.
   - Opens Android Storage settings for phone-wide cleanup.
   - Opens a selected app's App Info page because ordinary apps cannot silently
     clear another app's cache.

6. YouTube video control
   - Opens exact HTTPS YouTube links in the YouTube app when installed.
   - Searches YouTube for a named/specific video, even with shortened commands.
   - Keeps normal “open YouTube” app launching.

7. WhatsApp group summarization
   - Extracts Android MessagingStyle messages, sender names and group titles.
   - Groups messages by conversation and summarizes a named group or all recent
     WhatsApp notifications.
   - Works only with notification content Android exposes after Notification
     Access is granted; it does not read WhatsApp's private database or old chats.

8. Smart command recovery
   - Conservatively corrects common voice/typing mistakes and missing command
     structure around AURA's supported vocabulary.
   - Examples: opep→open, wataop→WhatsApp, grop→group,
     vollume→volume, vedio→video, sumarize→summarize,
     anslise→analyze, translet→translate, onscren→on screen,
     coolinig→cooling, catch clean→cache clean.
   - URLs, email addresses, long prose, codes and secrets are left untouched.

9. Smart-butler personality
   - Optional original dry, intelligent butler banter for normal device commands.
   - Levels: Off, Subtle and Playful.
   - No jokes are added to urgent, medical, payment, security, OTP, error,
     research or news responses.
   - Foreground chat and background wake-word replies use the same setting.

10. Existing v0.7 Analyzer capabilities retained
   - Encrypted notification vault and priority classification.
   - Gmail/Slack/Jira notification preview summaries.
   - Detailed current news and grounded research when configured.
   - Gallery image analysis, screen analysis, song finder and temporal memory.

Privacy and Android boundaries
- Screen capture always requires visible Android consent.
- Notification analysis requires Notification Access and begins after it is granted.
- Detailed vision/translation uses the configured Gemini provider when enabled.
- AURA never asks for WhatsApp/Gmail passwords and cannot bypass app encryption.
- Other apps' caches cannot be silently deleted by a normal third-party app.
- Cooling Assist reduces permitted power use; actual cooling is controlled by Android and hardware.
